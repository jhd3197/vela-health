# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- Two widgets for the Vela desk. **Habits today** shows how many of your habits
  you have ticked off today; **This week** lists each habit against its own
  weekly target. Both are published by Health itself and drawn by Vela, and both
  raise the rail's attention dot when a save has not gone through. Only your
  real habit records are published — never the clearly labelled sample metrics
  shown elsewhere in the app. The `widgets` permission is optional: refuse it
  and Health works exactly as before.
